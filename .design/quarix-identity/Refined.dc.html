03 — Shortlist

The two that survive scrutiny

Both are one operation on one form, both hold at 16px, and neither leans on a trend. They are shown here over their construction, then at display and at icon size.

01Quarter

The strongest silhouette of the five, and the only one whose idea is the name.

—The displaced quadrant reads as a tail without drawing one.
—Asymmetry makes it recognisable in outline, at any size.
—The gap widens and the ring thickens below 32px so it never fills in.
—Risk: the gap is the whole idea, so print and embroidery need the micro cut.
02Corner

Quieter and more forgiving, but a notched circle is well-trodden ground.

—One square corner does the work of a tail — nothing is added.
—Survives any reproduction; there is no gap to lose.
—Sits comfortably beside a geometric wordmark.
—Risk: less distinctive; several studios already own this shape.
