02 — Concepts

Five original directions for the Quarix symbol

Each is a single geometric operation on a closed form. No sparks, no nodes, no faces — every one is a Q first and a metaphor second.

01Quarter

A resolved ring with one quadrant stepped out along the diagonal.

Encodes the name. One measured move on a finished form — which is what a small studio actually does.

02Corner

A circle held everywhere except one square corner, where the tail would sit.

Craft meeting construction: the drawn curve and the built right angle in one silhouette.

03Module

The letter assembled from square modules on a five-by-five grid.

A design system and a compute grid read the same way. Systems thinking made visible.

04Handle

A ring with a solid selection handle seated on the tail diagonal.

Borrowed from the design tool, not from AI iconography. The mark of something being worked on.

05Chord

A ring cut by a straight bar that runs out past the edge.

A clean line taken through a closed form: the engineering pass that turns a shape into a product.
