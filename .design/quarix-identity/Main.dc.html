QUARIX

The idea is the name

A circle with one quadrant moved a quarter step out. Quarix is read back into its own mark rather than illustrated by a borrowed metaphor.

Design and engineering, in one move

The ring is the resolved form — the design. The displaced quadrant is the measured transform on it — the engineering. One operation, exactly specified, reversible.

One deliberate change

It is not a redesign of the circle. It is one deliberate change that makes the whole thing work, which is the honest description of what the studio sells.

Distinctive by construction

Almost every Q adds a stroke. This one removes and displaces a piece, so the silhouette is unlike the field and stays legible in outline.

Timeless, not futuristic

A ring and a quadrant. There is no rendering style, gradient or effect to date it.
