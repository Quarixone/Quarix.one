01 — Reference reading

What the reference does well, and what Quarix should take from it

The attached mark is studied here as a set of principles only. None of its shapes, letterforms or proportions are carried into the Quarix identity.

One idea, one accentA plain geometric wordmark carries the whole identity; a single small mark does all of the differentiating. Nothing else is asked to work.
Geometric letterformsCircular bowls, flat terminals, a tall x-height and a tight fit. The type is the structure, not decoration on top of it.
Mark subordinate to wordThe glyph sits at roughly six tenths of the wordmark's height, aligned to the cap line. It punctuates; it never competes.
Optical spacingThe gap between mark and word is set by eye against the letterform, not by a fixed box or a grid unit.
Negative space as materialThe ground is most of the composition. The lockup occupies a small, confident centre and is given room on every side.
Monochrome firstStrip the colour and the logo still holds. Colour is one note, added last, and removable without loss.
Carried over
  • —The restraint — one mark, one word, nothing else
  • —A symbol built from a single geometric operation
  • —Cap-line alignment and optical spacing
  • —Monochrome that survives every background
Deliberately left behind
  • —The lowercase heavy setting
  • —The star glyph and its accent colour
  • —The soft vignette ground
  • —Any borrowed shape or proportion
